Get your free ticket.

Two minutes to register, then pick the hands-on workshops you want a seat in.

26 November 2026 · 13:00–21:00 Hôtel Parc Belle-Vue · Luxembourg City
  1. 1Register Free, for everyone
  2. 2Pick your workshops Limited seats
  3. 3See you on 26 November From 13:00
Step 1 · Your ticket

One free ticket. The whole event.

Keynotes in the auditorium, the round table, the exhibitor area, the coffee breaks and the evening cocktail. No tiers, no hidden fees.

Register for free

Free registration. Limited capacity.

Your ticket includes:

  • All talks and keynotes
  • Exhibitor area, all afternoon
  • Coffee breaks and refreshments
  • Networking cocktail
  • Access to the hands-on workshops (seat booked in step 2)
Step 2 · Your workshops

Save a seat in the hands-on workshops.

Seven small-group workshops run one after another in Salle Vauban, alongside the talks. Seats are limited, so book each one you want once you have your ticket.

The workshop list needs JavaScript. See them on the programme.

Workshops run at the same time as some talks: check the programme

Got questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Is the event really free?

Yes. The event is completely free to attend. There are no ticket tiers, hidden fees or paid upgrades.

How do I join a workshop?

Register for the event first, then reserve a seat in each workshop you want from step 2 above. Workshops have limited seats, so a seat is only yours once it's booked.

Can I attend several workshops?

Yes. The workshops run one after another, never at the same time, so you can book as many as you like.

What does my ticket include?

Your ticket includes access to all talks, the exhibitor area, networking areas, refreshments and the evening cocktail. Workshops are included too, subject to a booked seat.

Can I attend only part of the event?

Yes. Your ticket remains valid throughout the event, from 1:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

What should I do if I can no longer attend?

Please cancel your registration or contact the event team so your place can be offered to someone else.
