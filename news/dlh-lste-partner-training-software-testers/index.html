DLH, LSTE Partner: A Lasting Commitment to Training Tomorrow’s Software Testers in Luxembourg

By the Digital Learning Hub · Published 17 November 2026

A partner of the Luxembourg Software Testing Event (LSTE), the Digital Learning Hub (DLH) continues its commitment to promoting software testing in Luxembourg. After hosting LSTE in 2024, the DLH keeps actively supporting the testing community, in particular through its Software Tester Track training programme, delivered by Q-Leap.

A partnership that helps software testing grow

The DLH and LSTE share a common ambition: to promote software testing careers, foster skills development and help software quality be recognised as a major issue in digital transformation.

This commitment takes shape in particular through the Software Tester Track programmes, developed and delivered by Q-Leap and its expert testing trainers.

For several years, these courses have helped new software testers emerge in Luxembourg, supporting participants as they build professional skills and prepare for the realities of the job.

Software Tester Track: a career-focused programme leading to ISTQB certification

Designed as a complete training for the software tester role, the Software Tester Track combines theory, hands-on practice and experience shared by industry professionals.

The course content, designed and taught by Q-Leap and its trainers, covers testing fundamentals, test design methods and techniques, professional tools and software quality assurance best practices.

The programme also prepares participants for the ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) certification, an international reference in software testing.

Through this collaboration, the DLH and Q-Leap are actively training a new generation of professionals and strengthening the testing ecosystem in the Grand Duchy.

Two new Software Tester Tracks announced for 2027

The journey continues in 2027 with two new training sessions at the Digital Learning Hub:

  • From 2 February 2027: Software Tester Track in English. An intensive 3-month programme for participants who want to acquire the essential skills of a software tester. The detailed calendar and programme will be published soon.
  • September 2027: Software Tester Track in French. A complete programme to discover and explore every dimension of the software tester role, including preparation for the ISTQB Foundation Level certification. Details and exact dates will be announced later.

Both sessions are new opportunities for anyone who wants to train, change careers or develop their skills in software quality in Luxembourg.

Let’s grow the testing community together!

Through its partnership with LSTE and the courses developed with Q-Leap, the DLH confirms its role in promoting digital skills and supporting future software testing professionals.

LSTE is pleased to count the Digital Learning Hub among its partners once again, and to highlight this concrete commitment to training, knowledge sharing and the growth of Luxembourg’s testing community.

See you at LSTE and in 2027 to keep growing software testing talent!
