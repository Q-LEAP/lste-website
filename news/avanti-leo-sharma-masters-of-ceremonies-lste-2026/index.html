Returning as Masters of Ceremonies: Avanti Sharma & Atreyam Leo Sharma

Published 23 November 2026

Some people make an event memorable. Others become part of its story.

We are truly delighted to welcome back Avanti Sharma and Atreyam Leo Sharma as our Masters of Ceremonies for the Luxembourg Software Testing Event (LSTE) 2026, taking place on 26 November 2026.

After sharing the stage with us last year, bringing their energy, enthusiasm, and wonderful chemistry to LSTE 2025, having them back this year feels like welcoming two members of our family home.

Because to us, Avanti and Leo are much more than Masters of Ceremonies.

They are part of our LSTE family.

They share our belief that software testing and quality engineering deserve greater visibility, recognition, and a meaningful place in the future of technology. They represent the curiosity, openness, and enthusiasm of a new generation, and remind us why sharing our passion for testing beyond our own professional circles matters so much.

Beyond LSTE, Avanti is actively involved in technology, innovation, and youth empowerment initiatives. Currently studying Chemical Engineering and Chemistry at Eindhoven University of Technology, she inspires younger generations through projects focused on artificial intelligence, STEM education, leadership, and sustainability.

Together with Leo, she brings something particularly valuable to our community: a fresh perspective, a genuine interest in what we do, and the ability to connect people across generations and backgrounds.

LSTE has always been about more than technology, methodologies, or tools. It is about people. About sharing knowledge, building connections, inspiring the next generation, and growing a community that believes in the value of quality.

And that is exactly why having Avanti and Leo with us again means so much.

Welcome back, Avanti and Leo! We are proud to have you by our side once again, not just on stage, but as part of the LSTE family and the story we continue to build together.

See you on 26 November 2026!
