Who we are

The meeting point for Luxembourg's QA community.

LSTE is Luxembourg's dedicated software testing event, bringing together testers, QA engineers, developers and technology leaders from Luxembourg, France, Belgium and Germany.

Designed for practitioners, LSTE focuses on real-world experience, practical insights and direct conversations between software testing professionals.

Not a general tech conference. One full day dedicated to software testing and quality.

Why come

What you'll experience at LSTE.

LSTE isn't a general tech conference, it's the one day a year Luxembourg's entire QA community gathers in one room: 400+ testers, engineers, and QA leads from banking, insurance, telecom, and the public sector. Here's what a day on the inside looks like.
