LSTE 2024

6th edition. 280+ registrations. A full day of testing.

The sixth Luxembourg Software Testing Event took place at the Digital Learning Hub in Esch-Belval, with hands-on tutorials in the morning, keynotes and talks in the afternoon, an exhibition area and a networking cocktail.
