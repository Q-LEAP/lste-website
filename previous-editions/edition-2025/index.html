LSTE 2025

400+ registrations. Nearly 300 participants. 60+ organisations.

On 27 November 2025, the Luxembourg Software Testing Event brought together professionals involved in software testing, quality assurance, digital transformation and software delivery, from hands-on practitioners to senior decision-makers, for a full day of insights, live demonstrations and networking.

LSTE connects the people building, managing and improving software quality across Luxembourg and the Greater Region.

Themes

What the community discussed

The topics that shaped the 2025 programme, each drawn from its sessions.

AI & quality engineering

Testing AI components at production scale; lessons from two years of adopting generative AI; AI for QA in ERP implementations.

Data quality & synthetic data

How synthetic data is changing testing, and a round table on data quality as the foundation of reliable AI.

Test automation

No-code test automation, end-to-end automation and the future of QA tooling, with live demonstrations.

Mobile & performance testing

Internal mobile device farms in CI, and scaling mobile performance testing with real devices and real conditions.

Testing in regulated environments

Automating end-to-end testing in highly regulated environments, including mobile 2FA in secured environments.

Research & QA practice

Advanced testing of cyber-physical systems from the SnT, and what it takes to build happy, high-impact QA teams.

The day

A full-day experience

LSTE is not a conference where people only sit and listen. In 2025 the day ran across a conference room, a demo room and an exhibition area.

Why it matters

More than a conference: a business and knowledge exchange

LSTE creates a focused environment where QA practitioners, IT leaders, technology providers and organisations facing real software quality challenges can meet, exchange experience and build new professional connections.

  • Exchange with peers who face the same software quality challenges in finance, insurance, the public sector and beyond.
  • Compare tools and approaches directly with solution providers on the exhibition floor.
  • Build connections across Luxembourg’s QA and software testing community in a single day.
