Thursday, 27 November 2025 · Luxembourg city

LSTE 2025.

The full programme of the Luxembourg Software Testing Event: keynotes in the Conference Room, demos and workshops in the Demo Room, and a networking cocktail. Master of Ceremony & moderator: Avanti Sharma.

The 2025 programme

The Luxembourg Software Testing Event took place on Thursday 27 November 2025 in Luxembourg city, running across two rooms: a Conference Room of keynotes and a Demo Room of demos and workshops, alongside an exhibition booth area from 13:00.

The day was hosted by Master of Ceremony and moderator Avanti Sharma, and closed with a networking cocktail at 19:00. The full schedule is below.
