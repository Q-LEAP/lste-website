From Abidjan to Casablanca, from Tunis to Luxembourg: Olivier Denoo, Our Guest of Honour at LSTE 2026

Published 25 November 2026

Casablanca, Abidjan, Lisbon, Tunis… Olivier Denoo travels the world to meet the different software testing communities, giving conference talks, speaking at events and taking part in international exchanges, including remotely with Rwanda.

After his latest stop in Tunisia, it is in Luxembourg that he sets down his bags this week.

Despite a particularly busy international schedule, Olivier is doing us the honour of joining us this Thursday, 26 November, to kick off the keynotes of the Luxembourg Software Testing Event (LSTE 2026).

An international ambassador for software testing

Vice-President of the ISTQB® (International Software Testing Qualifications Board) and President of the CFTL (Comité Français des Tests Logiciels), Olivier Denoo is a recognised figure in the international software testing community.

With nearly 30 years of experience in software quality assurance, he actively helps our discipline shine beyond borders.

His many trips and his exchanges with testing professionals around the world give him a particularly rich perspective on how our profession is changing.

At a time when artificial intelligence is turning our practices upside down, this international vision takes on its full meaning.

An opening keynote focused on the future of the tester

Fast & Furious – The Tester in 5 Years: AI Perspectives

What are the challenges, risks and opportunities of artificial intelligence? How do we reinvent ourselves in the age of augmented computing and this frantic race for speed? What will the tester’s job look like in five years?

With his characteristic humour and offbeat style, Olivier will invite us to explore these questions, drawing on nearly three decades of experience in software quality assurance.

His perspective is all the more interesting because it is shaped by his encounters with software testing communities around the world, facing common challenges in different technological, economic and cultural contexts.

Software testing has no borders

We are particularly happy and honoured that Luxembourg is part of this international journey.

Welcoming Olivier for the opening keynote of LSTE 2026 is a great opportunity for our community to benefit from his expertise, his experience and his vision of the future of our profession.

Thank you, Olivier, for making room for Luxembourg in your international schedule and for coming to share your expertise, your humour and your passion with our community!

See you this Thursday, 26 November, at LSTE 2026.
