Venue

Hôtel Parc Belle-Vue.

  • 5 Avenue Marie-Thérèse L-2132 Luxembourg City · Conference Center
  • 26 Nov 2026 · 13:00–21:00

In the heart of Luxembourg City, minutes from Hamilius by tram, bus or on foot.

Fully wheelchair accessible.

Get directions Public transport Parking information

Getting there

By car

  • No parking at the hotel.
  • Parkolux Monterey: 24b Av. Monterey, 2163 Luxembourg, 5 min walk to the Conference Center. More information
  • P+R Bouillon: park for free, then take bus 15 (~15 min) to the Rheinsheim stop, right in front of the Conference Center.
Directions to Parkolux Monterey

Public transport

  • Bus: Amélie stop, ~2 min walk
  • Tram & bus: Hamilius stop, ~4 min walk
  • Free across Luxembourg, no ticket needed
Plan your route on mobiliteit.lu

From the airport

  • Bus 16 or 29, or the tram, from Findel Airport to Hamilius
  • ~25 min, then a 4 min walk to the venue
  • Free, no ticket needed
