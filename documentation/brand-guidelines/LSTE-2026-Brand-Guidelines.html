// Usage

Three rules, that’s it

01

Keep clear space

Leave breathing room around the logo — at least the height of the “L” on every side.

02

Don’t distort it

Never stretch, tilt, recolour or add effects. Use the supplied files as they are.

03

Mind the contrast

Pick the version that stands out on your background: light on dark, ink on light.

Need something else?

Custom formats, print-ready files or partnership questions — reach out at hello@lste.lu.
